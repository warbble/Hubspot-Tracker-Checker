Common Questions

What if I get an "unsure" result?

This usually means your tracking code is present but something is preventing it from firing — often a cookie consent banner or JavaScript conflict. We can help you diagnose the exact issue.

How do I verify the Portal ID is mine?

In your HubSpot account, go to Settings → Account Management → Account Defaults. Your Hub ID is displayed at the top — it should match what we detect.

I have tracking on some pages but not others

This is more common than you'd think, especially with custom landing pages or third-party tools. Test multiple URLs to get the full picture.
